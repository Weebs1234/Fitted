What's new

Every version of Fitted CV, newest first. Open a version to see what changed.

Version 1.22.14 November 2026Latest
  • Google Gemini: Connect no longer fails when Gemini's free limit is used up. The key is saved as connected, and Fitted tells you the limit is reached for now.
  • Google Gemini: Fitted now picks Gemini's stable Flash model, and when that one is over its free limit, it automatically tries a lighter Gemini model so drafting keeps going.
  • The same error no longer shows twice.
Version 1.22.04 November 2026
  • Windows: changes so Microsoft Defender is less likely to mistake Fitted for malware. Updates now swap the app in place instead of running a hidden helper script, the app file carries its name, maker and version (Properties > Details), and it is built with its own starter code instead of the shared one that antivirus often flags.
  • Interview rounds per application. Open Details and notes and add each round: recruiter call, phone screen, first or second interview, assessment or final interview, with the date and who you meet. The next round shows on the row and in the Today panel ("Recruiter call tomorrow"), and goes into the calendar file.
  • Interview prep per round: the prep sheet focuses on the round you are in, so a recruiter call gets different questions than a final interview.
  • Write a reply (Pro): under More, paste what a recruiter or company sent you and get a short reply for LinkedIn, email or WhatsApp in your own CV's facts. Pick the tone (warm or formal) and the length. Copy it, or open it in your email app.
  • Phone: the Files and More menus no longer close when the page scrolls a little.
  • The applications list is saved more safely, so a save at the same moment as a read cannot break it.
Version 1.21.14 November 2026
  • Windows: background reminders are removed. Microsoft Defender mistook the daily reminder (a scheduled task that showed a notification through Windows PowerShell) for malware. Fitted no longer creates scheduled tasks or runs PowerShell, and the setting is switched off. On Windows, the Today panel in the app still shows follow-ups that are due and interviews coming up.
  • Mac and Android keep their reminders.
Version 1.21.04 November 2026
  • Today: a small panel in the sidebar (on phones at the top of Applications) with what needs doing now: an interview coming up, follow-ups that are due, and jobs still to apply for. Click one to go straight there. It also shows how many applications you sent this week, how your list of several jobs is doing, and now and then a tip you can hide.
  • Reminders, also when Fitted is closed: switch them on in the Today panel or in Settings → App. Every morning at 9, Fitted lets you know when a follow-up is due or an interview is today or tomorrow. Windows and Mac use their own scheduler; on Android, allow notifications when asked. Switching it off removes the daily check again.
Version 1.20.04 November 2026
  • Calmer Settings: four tabs instead of one long list. Your CV (languages, base CV, photo), AI, App (language, working faster, getting started) and Data and licence (moving to another device, licence, updates).
  • Calmer Applications: each job shows the one next step that fits its status (apply, follow up, prepare, add to calendar), with your files and the rest in two small menus, Files and More. The totals at the top are down to five, and the weekly goal is a single line until you set one.
  • Fixes: small link buttons (such as What's new and see the list) were unreadable in dark mode, and help text in Settings looked like headings. Secondary buttons now look like buttons.
Version 1.19.04 November 2026
  • Move to another device: Settings → Move to another device makes one backup file with your CV, photo, applications and every job folder. On your new computer or phone, choose Bring in a backup (also on the first screen) and everything is there. What was on that device is kept first, in the Backups folder. AI keys and your licence stay on the device they belong to.
  • Interviews in your calendar: when an application has an interview date, Add to calendar makes a calendar event with the company, role, contact and your interview prep, and a reminder the day before.
  • Cover letters as Word too: next to each cover letter PDF, Fitted now also makes a Word file (.docx), for sites that want everything in Word.
  • Where your interviews come from: Applications shows, per job site, how many applications you sent and how many led to an interview, from your own list.
Version 1.18.04 November 2026
  • Fitted now speaks Dutch. The app follows the language of your computer or phone: set to Dutch, every screen, button, message and the help page are in Dutch; otherwise English. Change it any time in Settings → Language of Fitted (Automatic, English or Nederlands).
  • Your CVs, cover letters and job ads are never changed by this: they stay in the languages you chose for your CV.
  • Dates in Applications follow the interface language too (for example "10 nov." in Dutch).
Version 1.17.03 November 2026
  • Quick start guide: a short guide with the main steps opens when Fitted starts. Tick "Don't show this again" to stop it; turn it back on, or open it any time, in Settings → Working faster.
  • Word copy of every CV: next to each PDF, Fitted now also makes the same CV as a Word file (.docx), for job sites and recruiters that ask for Word. It uses real headings and bullet lists, so application systems read it well, and includes your photo where you show one.
  • New in Fitted Pro: form answers. For a job in Applications (or right after building), tick the questions the application form asks or paste its own, and Fitted writes ready-to-paste answers from your CV only, with a character count for forms with a limit. Questions your CV can't answer (salary, start date, work permit) are left for you. The answers are saved with the job.
  • Copied a job link? On Windows and Mac, Fitted notices a link you copied when you switch back to it and offers to load the job or add it to your list. Only links are looked at and nothing is kept; switch it off in Settings → Working faster.
  • Weekly goal: set how many applications you aim for each week (handy for UWV). Applications shows how far you are this week, and the overview export now lists applications per week, with the goal.
  • Fix: two settings changed in quick succession could undo each other. Settings and your CV are now saved safely, one change at a time.
Version 1.16.13 November 2026
  • Opening another job from your list, starting over or pressing Ctrl+Shift+N now asks first when the page has work you haven't built yet, instead of clearing it.
  • Start over no longer marks a job from your list as built when you build a different job afterwards.
  • Several jobs at once: when the AI's per-minute limit is reached (common on free plans), Fitted waits a minute and tries again instead of skipping the draft.
  • A job from your list whose drafting failed now says so when you open it, with what to do next.
  • Follow-up dates and "Last 7 days" now use your own time zone, so a reminder is never a day early or late around midnight.
  • Follow-up emails now come in all 10 CV languages (Italian, Portuguese, Swedish, Danish and Norwegian were in English before), and interview prep PDFs have headings in every language.
Version 1.16.03 November 2026
  • Applications got smarter: search by company, role or notes, and filter by status, including "Follow up due". Click a number at the top to filter on it.
  • Details and notes per application: date applied, interview date, follow-up date, contact person and your own notes. They save by themselves and go into applications.xlsx too.
  • Follow-up reminders: when you set a job to Applied, Fitted notes the date and plans a follow-up a week later. Jobs to chase get an amber "Follow up due" mark.
  • Export an overview of a period (this week, last month, or your own dates) as a PDF or Excel file, with date, company, role, status, how you applied, contact and notes. Handy for your own records or for UWV. Files go to the new Reports folder.
  • New application warns you when you already have that job in Applications. Building again updates the same job.
  • Several jobs at once: paste up to 10 job links. Fitted loads each ad and drafts your lines in the background (and, with Pro, a cover letter if you like). You check and build them one after another; after each build, "Next job in your list" opens the next one.
  • Company and role are found more reliably, also on job sites that list a sector instead of the employer. When Fitted isn't sure, the field turns amber with "Check this".
  • Follow-up email: for jobs you applied to, one click makes a short, polite follow-up email in the language of your CV, ready to copy or open in your email app. "I sent it" notes it and plans the next reminder. The sidebar shows how many follow-ups are due.
  • New in Fitted Pro: match check. See which requirements in the ad your CV covers, partly covers or doesn't, with the fact from your CV that shows it. Nothing is counted without proof on your CV.
  • New in Fitted Pro: interview prep. For jobs you applied to or have an interview for: 8 likely questions with answer points taken only from your own CV, plus good questions to ask them. Saved as a PDF in the job's folder.
Version 1.15.03 November 2026
  • Faster when you apply for many jobs: paste a job link and the ad loads by itself, and drafting with AI starts straight after. Switch either off in Settings → Working faster.
  • "Next application" button after building clears the page and puts the cursor in the link field, ready for the next job.
  • Keyboard shortcuts (optional, on Windows and Mac): Ctrl+Enter builds, Ctrl+Shift+D drafts, Ctrl+Shift+N starts the next application, Alt+↓/↑ moves between roles, Ctrl+1/2/3 switches pages. Press ? to see them all, or turn them off in Settings.
  • A 1-minute tour for new users, offered on first start. Skip it any time with Skip tour or Esc; restart it from Help or Settings.
  • Applications shows a short how-to before your first application.
  • What's new: every version and its changes, on the Help page (also via Settings → Version).
  • With a cover letter, Fitted also makes one PDF with the letter first and the CV after it, for employers who ask for both in one file ("Letter + CV in one PDF").
  • Tidier Fitted folder: "output" is now "Applications", with one folder per job named by date, company and role (for example "2026-11-03 Acme - Team Lead"). Files have readable names, such as "Your Name - CV (NL).pdf" and "Your Name - Cover letter (NL).pdf", ready to send as they are. Existing folders move over by themselves, and your applications list follows.
  • Inside each job folder: the job ad as "Job ad.txt", "Editable copies" for a last tweak, and Fitted's own notes in "Details". CV backups now go to "Backups", previews to "Previews", and "About this folder.txt" explains what is where.
Version 1.14.01 November 2026
  • Clearer messages: confirmations now appear as a card in the free space of the sidebar (at the top on phones), with a colour and icon for done, needs you, and went wrong. They stay longer and have a close button. Error messages stay until read.
  • While Fitted works on something longer (loading a job ad, drafting, writing a cover letter, building), a "Working" card says what is happening.
  • Steps tick off: each step on New application gets a green tick when it's done.
  • A "ready to build" bar follows you down the page, saying what's left (for example "2 drafted roles to review") with a Build CVs button.
  • "Start over" now asks first when there's unsaved work on the page.
  • Small grey text is darker and easier to read.
Version 1.13.01 November 2026
  • New in Fitted Pro: cover letters. Draft a cover letter for each job ad, in every language of your CV, written only from what is on your CV. Read and edit it, confirm it is true, and it is built as a PDF in your CV style, next to your CVs.
  • Your CV keeps its own language (since 1.12): an imported German or Dutch CV becomes your base CV in that language. Change it in Settings → Base CV.
  • CV photo (since 1.12): add one in Settings and choose which languages show it. German CVs usually include one.
Version 1.12.01 November 2026
  • Your CV keeps its own language. A CV imported in German, Dutch or any other supported language becomes your base CV in that language; other languages are translated from it. Starting from scratch, you pick the language first.
  • Change your base CV any time in Settings → Base CV.
  • CV photo: add a photo in Settings and tick the languages whose CVs should show it (German is ticked by default). Fitted crops it to a portrait and places it at the top right in every CV style.
  • On the last step before building, Fitted reminds you that German CVs usually include a photo.

  • An editable copy of each CV is saved in the "editable" folder next to it, for a last tweak. Open it with the new "Editable copies" button after building.

Wat is er nieuw

Versie 1.22.14 november 2026Nieuwste
  • Google Gemini: Verbinden mislukt niet meer als de gratis limiet van Gemini op is. De sleutel wordt als verbonden opgeslagen en Fitted laat weten dat de limiet voorlopig bereikt is.
  • Google Gemini: Fitted kiest nu het stabiele Flash-model van Gemini, en als dat over zijn gratis limiet is, probeert het vanzelf een lichter Gemini-model zodat voorstellen maken doorgaat.
  • Dezelfde foutmelding verschijnt niet meer twee keer.
Versie 1.22.04 november 2026
  • Windows: aanpassingen zodat Microsoft Defender Fitted minder snel voor malware aanziet. Updates wisselen de app nu direct om in plaats van via een verborgen hulpscript, het app-bestand heeft een naam, maker en versie (Eigenschappen > Details), en het wordt gebouwd met eigen opstartcode in plaats van de gedeelde die antivirus vaak markeert.

Open Details en notities en voeg elke ronde toe: gesprek met recruiter, telefonische screening, eerste of tweede gesprek, assessment of laatste gesprek, met datum en met wie. De volgende ronde staat bij de sollicitatie en in het Vandaag-paneel ("Gesprek met recruiter morgen") en gaat mee in het agendabestand.
  • Gespreksvoorbereiding per ronde: de voorbereiding richt zich op de ronde waarin je zit, dus een gesprek met een recruiter krijgt andere vragen dan een laatste gesprek.
  • Antwoord schrijven (Pro): plak onder Meer wat een recruiter of bedrijf je stuurde en krijg een kort antwoord voor LinkedIn, e-mail of WhatsApp, met de feiten uit je eigen cv. Kies de toon (persoonlijk of zakelijk) en de lengte. Kopieer het of open het in je e-mailprogramma.
  • Telefoon: de menu's Bestanden en Meer sluiten niet meer als de pagina een stukje scrolt.
  • Het sollicitatieoverzicht wordt veiliger opgeslagen, zodat opslaan en lezen op hetzelfde moment het niet kapot kan maken.
