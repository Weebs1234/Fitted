What's new

Every version of Fitted CV, newest first. Open a version to see what changed.

Version 1.25.06 November 2026Latest
  • One CV unless you ask for more: Fitted makes the CV in your base language, or in the ad's language once an ad is loaded. The language chips above the ad are always there, so you can add another language for this job with one tap.
  • One review screen: check every changed line, your profile, your letter and the job details on one screen, each with its own tick, then build. No more clicking through step after step.
  • See what is left out: when your tailored line already says what one of the job's points says, that point shows crossed out ("Left out: your line already says this"), on the tailoring screen and in the review.
  • Find repeats in My CV: one click shows points in the same job that say the same thing, and you choose which one to remove. Nothing changes until you choose.
  • AI over its free limit? The message now has an "Add a free backup AI" button that takes you straight to it. With a backup, Fitted switches by itself next time.
  • A pasted job ad starts drafting by itself, the same way a fetched one does, and the match check (Pro) follows straight after.
  • After building, the button says "Build again", so it's clear your CV is already made.
  • Windows: dates show the way your language writes them (06-11-2026 in Dutch).
Version 1.24.26 November 2026
  • No more saying the same thing twice: when the tailored line for a job already says what one of that job's points says, Fitted leaves that point out of the CV. A point that adds anything of its own (a number, a tool, a result) always stays, and your saved CV doesn't change.
Version 1.24.16 November 2026
  • No more question marks in your PDFs: special hyphens, spaces and quotes that an AI sometimes writes (as in "contact-centre" or "large-scale") now print as normal characters in your CV, cover letter and interview prep.
  • Via a recruiter: tick "Via a recruiter" under Company (Fitted ticks it for you when the ad says "our client") and fill in the agency, plus the employer if the ad names it. Your applications list then shows "Holidu (via Hirewise)" or "Hirewise (recruiter)".
Version 1.24.05 November 2026
  • CVs only in the language of the ad: when you load a job ad, Fitted picks its language (for example only German for a German ad) and drafts, writes and builds just that CV. Click another language above the ad to add it for this job.
  • A calmer result after building: one row for the CV you send (open the CV, the letter, or both in one PDF), and the other languages, Word files and the rest in three small menus.
  • Your other languages now follow your base CV. When you save a change under My CV, Fitted translates only what you changed into your other languages straight away. Phone numbers, email addresses and links are copied as they are, and edits you made yourself in another language stay. Any change now counts, not only an added job or line.
  • Each language tab under My CV has a "Translate again" link, for a translation that no longer matches.
  • Delete an application: under More, "Delete application" removes it from your list after you confirm. Its CVs, letter and notes go to the Deleted folder in Fitted, so you can still get them back.
Version 1.23.15 November 2026
  • Connect now tries up to 8 of a service's models until one answers, for every AI (it did this only for Google Gemini). Services such as Cerebras also list models that need a paid plan; Fitted now skips those and saves the first one that works.
Version 1.23.05 November 2026
  • Backup AIs: in Settings → AI, add up to two backup AIs, such as Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter (all have a free option). When your AI reaches its free limit, runs out of credit or is very busy, Fitted switches to the next one by itself and tells you which one did the job. Your main AI and the one for translations also step in for each other.
  • Groq: when its free per-minute limit is reached, Fitted waits a moment and tries again, and when one Groq model is used up it tries a lighter Groq model, the same way it already did for Google Gemini.
  • More AI choices: Mistral (European, with a free plan), Cerebras (free allowance, very fast), OpenAI and DeepSeek (paid per use), and LM Studio as a second free local option next to Ollama.
  • A clear message when your Claude API account has no credit: a Claude.ai subscription (Pro or Max) does not include API credit, which is billed separately.
  • The model list no longer shows voice models that can't write text (such as Orpheus on Groq).
Version 1.22.45 November 2026
  • CVs read better in applicant tracking systems (ATS): in the Classic and Elegant styles, the spaced-out section headings (such as WORK EXPERIENCE) are now real words to the software that reads your PDF, instead of separate letters. They look the same as before.
Version 1.22.34 November 2026
  • Google Gemini: Connect no longer stops at a model Gemini lists but won't run ("does not know that model"). It tries the next Gemini model until one answers, starting with Gemini 2.5 Flash, and drafting does the same.
Version 1.22.24 November 2026
  • Google Gemini: when Gemini is overloaded ("high demand"), Fitted now tries another Gemini model, still connects your key, and says in plain words that Gemini is busy, instead of showing a block of code.
  • Other AI errors show only the service's own message.
Version 1.22.14 November 2026
  • Google Gemini: Connect no longer fails when Gemini's free limit is used up. The key is saved as connected, and Fitted tells you the limit is reached for now.
  • Google Gemini: Fitted now picks Gemini's stable Flash model, and when that one is over its free limit, it automatically tries a lighter Gemini model so drafting keeps going.
  • The same error no longer shows twice.
Version 1.22.04 November 2026
  • Windows: changes so Microsoft Defender is less likely to mistake Fitted for malware. Updates now swap the app in place instead of running a hidden helper script, the app file carries its name, maker and version (Properties > Details), and it is built with its own starter code instead of the shared one that antivirus often flags.
  • Interview rounds per application. Open Details and notes and add each round: recruiter call, phone screen, first or second interview, assessment or final interview, with the date and who you meet. The next round shows on the row and in the Today panel ("Recruiter call tomorrow"), and goes into the calendar file.
  • Interview prep per round: the prep sheet focuses on the round you are in, so a recruiter call gets different questions than a final interview.
  • Write a reply (Pro): under More, paste what a recruiter or company sent you and get a short reply for LinkedIn, email or WhatsApp in your own CV's facts. Pick the tone (warm or formal) and the length. Copy it, or open it in your email app.
  • Phone: the Files and More menus no longer close when the page scrolls a little.
  • The applications list is saved more safely, so a save at the same moment as a read cannot break it.
Version 1.21.14 November 2026
  • Windows: background reminders are removed. Microsoft Defender mistook the daily reminder (a scheduled task that showed a notification through Windows PowerShell) for malware. Fitted no longer creates scheduled tasks or runs PowerShell, and the setting is switched off. On Windows, the Today panel in the app still shows follow-ups that are due and interviews coming up.
  • Mac and Android keep their reminders.
Version 1.21.04 November 2026
  • Today: a small panel in the sidebar (on phones at the top of Applications) with what needs doing now: an interview coming up, follow-ups that are due, and jobs still to apply for. Click one to go straight there. It also shows how many applications you sent this week, how your list of several jobs is doing, and now and then a tip you can hide.
  • Reminders, also when Fitted is closed: switch them on in the Today panel or in Settings → App. Every morning at 9, Fitted lets you know when a follow-up is due or an interview is today or tomorrow. Windows and Mac use their own scheduler; on Android, allow notifications when asked. Switching it off removes the daily check again.
Version 1.20.04 November 2026
  • Calmer Settings: four tabs instead of one long list. Your CV (languages, base CV, photo), AI, App (language, working faster, getting started) and Data and licence (moving to another device, licence, updates).
  • Calmer Applications: each job shows the one next step that fits its status (apply, follow up, prepare, add to calendar), with your files and the rest in two small menus, Files and More. The totals at the top are down to five, and the weekly goal is a single line until you set one.
  • Fixes: small link buttons (such as What's new and see the list) were unreadable in dark mode, and help text in Settings looked like headings. Secondary buttons now look like buttons.
Version 1.19.04 November 2026
  • Move to another device: Settings → Move to another device makes one backup file with your CV, photo, applications and every job folder. On your new computer or phone, choose Bring in a backup (also on the first screen) and everything is there. What was on that device is kept first, in the Backups folder. AI keys and your licence stay on the device they belong to.
  • Interviews in your calendar: when an application has an interview date, Add to calendar makes a calendar event with the company, role, contact and your interview prep, and a reminder the day before.
  • Cover letters as Word too: next to each cover letter PDF, Fitted now also makes a Word file (.docx), for sites that want everything in Word.
  • Where your interviews come from: Applications shows, per job site, how many applications you sent and how many led to an interview, from your own list.
Version 1.18.04 November 2026

Version 1.17.03 November 2026

For a job in Applications (or right after building), tick the questions the application form asks or paste its own, and Fitted writes ready-to-paste answers from your CV only, with a character count for forms with a limit. Questions your CV can't answer (salary, start date, work permit) are left for you. The answers are saved with the job.

Wat is er nieuw

Elke versie van Fitted CV, nieuwste eerst. Open een versie om te zien wat er veranderde. (De wijzigingen zelf staan in het Engels, zoals in de app.)

Versie 1.25.06 november 2026Nieuwste
  • Eén cv, tenzij je meer wilt: Fitted maakt het cv in je basistaal, of in de taal van de vacature zodra die geladen is. De taalknoppen boven de vacature staan er altijd, dus met één tik voeg je een andere taal toe voor deze baan.
  • Eén controlescherm: controleer elke gewijzigde regel, je profiel, je brief en de vacaturegegevens op één scherm, elk met een eigen vinkje, en maak dan je cv. Niet meer stap voor stap doorklikken.
  • Zie wat wegvalt: zegt je afgestemde regel al wat een van de punten bij die baan zegt, dan staat dat punt doorgestreept ("Weggelaten: je regel zegt dit al"), bij het afstemmen en bij het controleren.
  • Herhalingen zoeken in Mijn cv: met één klik zie je punten bij dezelfde baan die hetzelfde zeggen, en jij kiest welke weg mag. Er verandert niets tot je kiest.
  • AI aan zijn gratis limiet? In de melding staat nu een knop "Gratis reserve-AI toevoegen" die je er meteen heen brengt. Met een reserve schakelt Fitted de volgende keer vanzelf over.
  • Een geplakte vacature start vanzelf met voorstellen, net als een opgehaalde, en de matchcheck (Pro) volgt direct daarna.
  • Na het maken heet de knop "Opnieuw maken", zodat duidelijk is dat je cv al klaar is.
  • Windows: datums staan zoals jouw taal ze schrijft (06-11-2026 in het Nederlands).
Versie 1.24.26 november 2026
  • Niets meer dubbel: als de afgestemde regel bij een baan al zegt wat een van de punten bij die baan zegt, laat Fitted dat punt weg uit het cv. Een punt dat iets eigens toevoegt (een getal, een tool, een resultaat) blijft altijd staan, en je opgeslagen cv verandert niet.
Versie 1.24.16 november 2026
  • Geen vraagtekens meer in je pdf's: speciale streepjes, spaties en aanhalingstekens die een AI soms schrijft (zoals in "contact-centre" of "large-scale") komen nu als gewone tekens in je cv, brief en gespreksvoorbereiding.
  • Via een recruiter: vink onder Bedrijf "Via een recruiter" aan (Fitted doet dat zelf als de vacature "onze opdrachtgever" zegt) en vul het bureau in, en de werkgever als de vacature die noemt. In je overzicht staat dan "Holidu (via Hirewise)" of "Hirewise (recruiter)".
Versie 1.24.05 november 2026
  • Cv's alleen in de taal van de vacature: laad je een vacature, dan kiest Fitted de taal ervan (bijvoorbeeld alleen Duits bij een Duitse vacature) en stelt het alleen dat cv op, schrijft en maakt het. Klik boven de vacature op een andere taal om die voor deze vacature toe te voegen.
  • Een rustiger resultaat na het maken: één rij voor het cv dat je verstuurt (cv, brief, of beide in één pdf openen), en de andere talen, Word-bestanden en de rest in drie kleine menu's.
  • Je andere talen volgen nu je basis-cv. Sla je onder Mijn cv een wijziging op, dan vertaalt Fitted alleen wat je veranderde meteen in je andere talen. Telefoonnummers, e-mailadressen en links worden gewoon overgenomen, en aanpassingen die je zelf in een andere taal deed blijven staan. Elke wijziging telt nu mee, niet alleen een extra baan of regel.
  • Elk taaltabblad onder Mijn cv heeft een link "Opnieuw vertalen", voor een vertaling die niet meer klopt.
  • Een sollicitatie verwijderen: onder Meer haalt "Sollicitatie verwijderen" hem uit je overzicht nadat je dat bevestigt. De cv's, brief en notities gaan naar de map Deleted in Fitted, dus je kunt ze nog terughalen.

  • Verbinden probeert nu tot 8 modellen van een dienst tot er een antwoordt, bij elke AI (eerst alleen bij Google Gemini). Diensten zoals Cerebras tonen ook modellen waarvoor je moet betalen; Fitted slaat die nu over en bewaart het eerste model dat werkt.

Bereikt je AI zijn gratis limiet, is het tegoed op of is hij erg druk, dan schakelt Fitted vanzelf over naar de volgende en laat weten welke het werk deed.
