A recruiter likes your CV. The next thing they do is look you up on LinkedIn. If the job titles, dates or companies don't match, doubt creeps in. If they match and the profile adds a bit more, you look even better.

## What must match

- **Job titles.** Use the same title on both. If your official title was odd, pick one clear version and use it everywhere.
- **Employers and dates.** Month and year, the same on both. Small differences look careless; big ones look like you're hiding something.
- **Education and certificates.** Same names, same years.
- **Your name and contact details.** Obvious, but check the email address and phone number.

## What can differ

Your CV is tailored to each job; your LinkedIn profile is for everyone. That's fine:

- **The lines under each job.** Your CV shows the two or three points that fit this job. LinkedIn can show a slightly broader picture.
- **Your headline.** On LinkedIn, describe what you do and want: "Operations manager · logistics and customer service · open to roles in the Netherlands". On your CV, the profile is aimed at one role.
- **Extras.** LinkedIn has room for recommendations, posts, volunteering and a photo. Your CV doesn't need them.

## A 20-minute checklist

1. Put your CV next to your profile and check every title, employer and date.
2. Rewrite your LinkedIn headline: role + what you're good at + what you're looking for.
3. Write a short About section (four or five sentences): what you do, two highlights, what you want next.
4. Under each job, add three or four lines with real results, the same facts as on your CV.
5. Add your top skills, matching the words employers use in the ads you're applying to.
6. Turn on "Open to work" (visible to recruiters only, if you prefer).
7. Ask one or two former colleagues for a short recommendation.

## Keep everything true

Recruiters compare. If your CV says "led a team of 40" and LinkedIn says "team member", they'll ask. The safest rule: never put anything on either one that you couldn't explain in an interview.

## The short version

Same facts everywhere, a sharper focus on your CV, a broader picture on LinkedIn. Check them side by side every time you change jobs or update your CV.
