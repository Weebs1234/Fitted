From today you can download Fitted CV from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N3J1XHVJQ75). One click to install, and the Store keeps the app up to date for you.

## What is Fitted CV?

Fitted CV tailors your CV to every job ad, using only what you really did. Paste a job ad, Fitted drafts one line per job that matches a real requirement, you confirm every change, and you get a ready-to-send PDF in each of your languages. Every job goes into your applications list, with follow-ups, interview rounds and the Today panel.

## Why the Store?

- **One click:** search "Fitted CV" in the Microsoft Store, or use [this link](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N3J1XHVJQ75).
- **Checked by Microsoft:** the app is tested and signed by Microsoft, so no warnings when you install it.
- **Always up to date:** new versions arrive automatically through the Store.

## Try it free

Your first 3 jobs are free, with every Pro feature: up to 3 tailored lines per job, your profile and skills per ad, a cover letter, form answers, interview prep per round and short replies to recruiters.

## Your data stays with you

Your CV and files stay on your own computer, not on a Fitted server. You choose which AI to use, including free options, or a local AI where nothing leaves your computer.

Questions? Email help@getfitted.nl. And read the [user guide](../guide.html) to get off to a good start.
