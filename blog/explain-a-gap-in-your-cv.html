How to explain a gap in your CV (without over-explaining)

Time out of work for care, health, study, a move or a job search? How to show it on your CV, what to say in the interview, and what to leave out.

Almost everyone has a gap somewhere. You cared for a parent, raised kids, moved country, got ill, studied, or simply took longer than planned to find the next job. A gap isn't a problem in itself. A gap that looks like you're hiding something can be.

Show it, don't hide it

Leaving out dates or only listing years ("2019 – 2022") to blur a gap rarely works. Recruiters notice, and then they wonder why. It's better to be clear and short.

Put the gap on your CV as its own line, in the same place as a job would go:

  • 2023 – 2024 · Career break: caring for a family member
  • 2022 · Relocation from the UK to the Netherlands
  • Mar – Oct 2025 · Job search and Dutch language course

One line is enough. You don't owe anyone the details.

Add what you did, if it's relevant

If you learned something or kept your skills up, add it underneath, just like a job:

  • Completed an online Excel and Power BI course
  • Organised the move, paperwork and school registration for a family of four
  • Volunteered weekly at the local food bank, planning the Saturday shift

Only add things that are true and that you're happy to talk about. "Took time to recover" is also a perfectly good reason, and needs no further explanation on paper.

In the interview: three sentences

If they ask, keep your answer short and point forward:

  1. What happened, in one sentence. "I took a year off to care for my father."
  2. What's different now, in one sentence. "That's settled now, and I'm fully available."
  3. Why this job, in one sentence. "And I'm looking for a role where I can use my planning experience again, which is why this one caught my eye."

Then stop talking. Long explanations make a gap sound bigger than it is.

What you don't have to share

You don't have to give medical details, talk about your family situation, or justify every month. In the Netherlands, an employer isn't allowed to ask about your health during the application process, so "for health reasons, now fully resolved" is enough.

The short version

Name the gap in one line, add anything useful you did, and prepare three calm sentences for the interview. A clear gap says "this person is honest". A hidden one says "what else is missing?"
